About Adlync

Sri Lanka's Smart Classified Ads & Marketplace

Our Mission

Adlync is a web-based classified advertising platform designed to help buyers and sellers connect effortlessly. Whether you're selling a vehicle, finding a new home, or searching for electronics, Adlync makes it easy to post, browse, and manage ads securely.

We aim to provide a secure, user-friendly interface where everyone can find the best deals with trust and convenience.

Why Choose Adlync?

Secure & Verified

All users and ads are verified for a safe marketplace experience.

Mobile Friendly

Responsive design and PWA features for a seamless mobile experience.

Ratings & Reviews

Buyers can rate sellers to build trust within the community.

Real-Time Chat

Instant communication between buyers and sellers for faster deals.
